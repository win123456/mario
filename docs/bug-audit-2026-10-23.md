# Music adjustment and bug audit — 2026-10-23

## Changes

- Music peak gain increases from `0.018` to `0.022` (about 22%, or 1.74 dB). Sound effects retain their existing `0.055` gain.
- Fixed a reproducible crash when multiple enemies or fireballs dealt a fatal hit in the same update. Health could go negative, causing an invalid array length in the hearts display. Damage now stops after game over, health bottoms out at zero, and updates stop before further hits or finish-line transitions.
- Fixed a reproducible start failure when Web Audio is missing, creation is denied, or resuming the audio context throws. Audio initialization now fails safely while the game remains playable; rejected resume promises are handled.

## Verification

`npm test` covers walking, jumping, landing, collectibles, tie combat, pause/restart, damage, all three level transitions, victory, moving platforms and attached stars, stable decorative flowers, cloud/tunnel routes, lava, mobile pipe entry and held down input, Retina buffers, sprite aspect ratio, and missing-image fallbacks.

New regression cases cover music versus effect gain, muting, missing/denied audio, two simultaneous fatal enemy collisions, two simultaneous fireball hits, and a fatal collision at the finish (which must not award a clear).

Browser checks used a local fixture with one remaining heart and two overlapping enemies. The game-over screen appeared, restart worked, and no console errors were recorded. A second fixture removed Web Audio constructors; start, movement, jump, and collectible feedback still worked without console errors. These fixture pages are not published.

This verifies the tested cases, not every possible input sequence or physical mobile browser. Gain was checked programmatically; perceived loudness still depends on device volume and speakers.
